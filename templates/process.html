How we deliver

Clear stages. Real-world outcomes.

Every service follows a rigorous path from the operational question to a solution your team can run with confidence.

Computer vision inspection

Make every frame a quality decision.

VISION INSPECTIONML & ANOMALYSOFTWARE & APPS
01 / LINE STUDY

Understand the failure modes

We observe the product, existing quality checks, throughput and conditions around the station. Together we define what a good part, a bad part and an actionable decision mean.

02 / OPTICAL DESIGN

Make the defect visible

We select camera position, lens, lighting and trigger strategy. This transforms the variability of a physical scene into reliable visual information.

03 / MODEL & VALIDATE

Train against real variation

We build the inspection logic with representative production data, test it against acceptance criteria and document performance before deployment.

04 / DEPLOY & IMPROVE

Integrate into the line

The system connects to controls and traceability systems. We monitor results, review exceptions and improve as new product variation arrives.

Machine learning & anomaly detection

Find the signal without requiring every failure example.

01 / DATA AUDIT

Map the available evidence

We identify data sources, data quality, labels and the operational metric the model must meaningfully improve.

02 / BASELINE

Establish normal behaviour

We test model approaches that learn normal images, sensor patterns or transactions, then detect deviations with a useful confidence threshold.

03 / HUMAN REVIEW

Design the exception loop

The right people can review, approve and feed edge cases back into the model, keeping the intelligence accountable and current.
